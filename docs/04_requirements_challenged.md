# Horizon ERP - Requirements I Challenged

A consultant's job includes saying when a requirement, taken literally, would hurt the client. Each
row names the requirement as written, why it is a problem on ERPNext v15 / for Horizon, and what was
proposed or built instead. None of these were dropped silently; all were discussed with the lead.

| # | Requirement as written | Why it is a problem | What I proposed / built instead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Enforce PO -> Receipt -> Inspection -> GRN; only GRN submission updates inventory" (a separate GRN step) | ERPNext's Purchase Receipt already is the goods receipt note: it writes the stock ledger, carries valuation and landed cost, feeds returns and the Purchase Invoice. A fourth document would duplicate all of that and break `pr_required`. | Purchase Receipt **is** the GRN. Inspection is forced before submit (Stock Settings *Stop* if QI missing or rejected), goods land in `HQ QC Hold - HML`, accepted quantity is auto-released to Raw Materials and rejected quantity goes to `HQ Rejected - HML` with a draft Purchase Return and Debit Note (`purchase_receipt.on_submit_purchase_receipt`, ADR-2). |
| 2 | "Create a Quotation DocType" / "Design and implement Delivery Note DocType" / "Design Purchase Receipt DocType" | New DocTypes with these names would collide with, or sit beside, the standard ones and lose pricing rules, taxes, GST e-invoicing, stock postings and every standard report. | Standard DocTypes extended with Custom Fields on a "Horizon" tab and `doc_events` hooks (`<module>/custom/<doctype>.json`, ADR-3 / ADR-15). The requested fields exist (availability, lead days, expected delivery date, signature, deviation notes, QC result, rejection reason). |
| 3 | "Auto-create Work Order from Sales Order for made-to-stock items; manual creation for make-to-order" | Reversed: make-to-stock is produced to a stock level, make-to-order is produced because an order exists. Auto-creating a WO per Sales Order for spares would fragment production into order-sized lots and ignore the stock already on the shelf. | Equipment (make-to-order) flows Sales Order -> Production Plan -> Work Order; spares (make-to-stock, seasonal) flow reorder level -> Material Request -> Production Plan. The capacity check applies to both (ADR-4). |
| 4 | "Tax rates by item category: Equipment 5 %, Spares 12 %, Components 5 %" | The 12 % slab was withdrawn in the September 2025 GST rate rationalisation (most 12 % goods moved to 5 % or 18 %), and in any case GST rate follows the HSN of the item, not the commercial category; pump parts and conveyor parts under 8413 / 8428 attract different rates. | Rates set up exactly as the brief states so the demo matches it (`Item Tax Template` GST 5 % / 12 % / 18 % on the Item Groups), with HSN codes on every item and a written caveat in `demo_data.py` and `data/README.md`: **rates to be confirmed per HSN by the client's CA before go-live**. Changing a rate is a one-line edit in `ITEM_GROUPS`. |
| 5 | "GSTR-2 summary (inward supplies)" | GSTR-2 has not been filed since 2017; inward credit is reconciled against the auto-drafted GSTR-2B. | GSTR-1 via India Compliance; inward side via India Compliance's Purchase Reconciliation Tool against GSTR-2B. No custom GSTR-2 report built. |
| 6 | "GST registration HQ Mumbai 27AABCU9602H1Z0" | The number fails the GSTIN mod-36 check digit; India Compliance refuses to save it on an Address, so no transaction could be posted. | Used `27AABCU9602H1Z9` (same PAN, valid check digit) and `07AABCU9602H1ZB` for Delhi (`demo_data.COMPANY_ADDRESSES`); raised as a client data issue - in a real project this would be confirmed from the GST certificate, not corrected by the consultant. Also became the first row of the Data Validation Report story. |
| 7 | "Log findings to Integration Log" | No DocType of that name exists in Frappe / ERPNext v15. *Integration Request* is for outbound calls; *Scheduled Job Log* already records every scheduler run. | Per-run record: Scheduled Job Log (standard). Failures: Error Log + alert to System Managers. Per-document findings: Info Comments on the affected Item / Purchase Order so the finding sits where the user works. Every job returns a summary dict for `bench execute` and tests (`horizon_core/jobs.py`). |
| 8 | "Finance wants to invoice Friday to meet Q4 target" (invoice before shipping) | Invoicing goods that failed inspection recognises revenue before control transfers (Ind AS 115 bill-and-hold tests are not met) and triggers GST on a supply that has not happened. The invoice would be reversed by the auditor. | *Invoice Now Ship Later* exists as an explicit, Finance-Director-approved option on the Shipment Decision so the trade-off is visible and logged, but the default remains invoice-from-Delivery-Note (`dn_required = Yes` plus the `validate_sales_invoice` gate with a logged FD bypass). Memo 3 documents the accounting caution. |
| 9 | Scope implied by "8-12 hours" | The brief lists roughly 110 numbered requirements across 20 areas plus four strategic memos and two bonus designs; building all of it to production quality in 8-12 hours is not credible, and pretending otherwise would hide where corners were cut. | Tiered delivery, stated openly in the requirement matrix (`01_configuration_design.md` section 5): **Tier 1 built and tested** (structure, approvals, QC gates, capacity, Production Audit, Shipment Decision, API, schedulers, quality metrics, bank aging, budget forecast, 19 reports, 6 workspaces); **Tier 2 configured** (payroll, leave, assets, budgets, bank); **Tier 3 Designed only** (Quarterly Purchase Plan logic, Budget Override application, bank sign-off lock, inter-branch GST document, portal, BI). |
| 10 | "Depreciation method: SLM, WDV, units of production" | ERPNext v15 Asset supports Straight Line, Double Declining Balance, Written Down Value and Manual; there is no units-of-production schedule. | SLM and WDV categories configured (Production Equipment SLM 180 months, Vehicles WDV 15 %, IT Equipment SLM 36 months) with automatic depreciation journals. For usage-based machines: method *Manual* with a monthly Depreciation Schedule computed from Job Card machine hours - documented as a Phase 2 item, not faked. |
| 11 | "Shift 2 PM - 10 PM" on the Workstation | ERPNext's Workstation working-hours overlap check is inclusive at the boundary, so 14:00-22:00 is rejected after 06:00-14:00. | Workstation windows 06:00-14:00 and 14:01-22:00; HRMS Shift Types keep 14:00-22:00 for attendance (ADR-12). |
| 12 | "Group Material Requests by supplier for bulk ordering" | ERPNext's reorder job groups by company and request type; supplier is an item default, not a Material Request attribute. | Kept the standard reorder (it is tested and respects `auto_indent`); supplier grouping happens one step later when the buyer pulls Material Requests into a Purchase Order per default supplier. Flagged as a partial fit rather than re-implementing reorder. |
| 13 | Separate "Quality Inspection DocType" in two sections with different status sets (Pass/Fail/Conditional Pass for receipts, Pass/Fail/Rework for manufacturing) | Two inspection DocTypes would split supplier and in-process quality history and break the Supplier Quality Scorecard. | One standard Quality Inspection with a custom `qc_result` select covering all four outcomes; the hook branches on the reference type (Purchase Receipt vs Job Card / Stock Entry). |
